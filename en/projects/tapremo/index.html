Questions and answers

Does my phone need IR?

Your phone needs a built-in infrared (IR) emitter to send TV commands. Without IR, you can explore the catalog and prepare routines.

Does it work with newer TVs?

It can control older and newer TVs that accept infrared commands and have a compatible profile. Test a remote on your own TV to choose one that works.

What is a routine or macro?

A saved sequence of remote buttons that runs in the order you choose. You control the buttons, repeat counts and delays between steps.

Does importing a routine run it on my TV?

Opening a file sends no commands. Review the steps for your own remote and save them first. You decide when to run the routine.
