Kelime. Atlas. İmla.

Play a free Turkish word game. Explore Türkiye’s geography with KTO Atlas and practice Turkish spelling with KTO İmla. A web game developed by Erkan Pulat with ODAKOD Yazılım.

01 / DISCOVERY

Three ways to be curious.

Word play

Build words from the given letters. Race against time, earn points and improve your personal best.

Atlas

Explore Türkiye’s mountains and 81 provinces. Reinforce your knowledge with location, formation and region challenges.

İmla

Separate or joined? Capitals, apostrophes, suffixes and conjunctions: practice in short rounds and learn why an answer is right.

02 / THE ORIGIN

A game became a place to learn.

An interest in word games led to an experiment that let livestream viewers compete through words. KTO brought the idea to a web game.

Atlas and İmla extend that same curiosity into geography and Turkish. An experience where you learn why an answer is right, beyond simply seeing a result.

03 / PATHS TO EXPLORE

01

Atlas · Put knowledge on the map.

Find mountains, distinguish their formation types and explore Türkiye’s provinces. Hints and explained answers help you prepare for the next round.

BEHIND THE SCENES

Today, challenge your personal best.

The live single-player word game runs offline and saves progress only on this device. Online and friend modes are currently disabled. The project’s server side was built with NestJS, Socket.IO and MongoDB; Atlas and İmla extend the learning experience.
