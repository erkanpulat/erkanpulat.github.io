02 / THE ORIGIN

Tuning a radio should be simple.

During military service, I looked for an answer to a simple question: “Which station broadcasts on which frequency here?” That search through scattered information became a guide anyone could use.

I organized public records around cities, districts and stations. Angular and static page generation turned this data into a fast, searchable and shareable experience.

03 / ANDROID

The Radyotara Android app.

The Radyotara web guide and Google Play Android app are two editions of the same frequency guide. Find frequencies in Türkiye, save favorites on your device, select your city by location and switch between light and dark themes.
