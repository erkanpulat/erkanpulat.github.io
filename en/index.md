## Journey and experience

### The First Planet — İnönü University · Computer Engineering

My first expedition took me to İnönü University. This is where I realized the software universe was far bigger than I had imagined.

Algorithms, data structures, software engineering, machine learning… Each was a different galaxy waiting to be explored. Every time I charted one, another appeared on the horizon. With every discovery, the universe grew a little larger.

And it was right here that my curiosity found its direction. I realized that what tied me to this journey wasn't just learning; it was turning what I discovered into solutions for real problems.

### Transformation — Cubicl · METU Teknokent

This time, what lay ahead weren't courses or assignments. There were real problems waiting to be solved, teammates who worked alongside me and trusted me, and thousands of users relying on the systems my code brought to life. This planet had rules of its own: every line I wrote had a consequence, every decision a cost.

This planet was called Cubicl.

I didn't just write software here. I took responsibility at every stage of a product's journey — from the first idea to meeting real users and evolving over time. This is where I learned that good engineering isn't just writing code; it's owning a system end to end, growing it, and keeping it reliably on its feet.

### My Own Stars — Evolution

For the first time, there was no ready-made route in front of me. Every star that would shine in this universe would be shaped by my own decisions.

This time I turned years of experience into ideas of my own. I built every layer myself — from architecture to user experience, from server infrastructure to running live systems. Every new star was a reflection of everything I had learned on the journeys before it.

Along the way, I kept building freelance projects to solve real needs across different industries, and deepened my perspective with courses taught by experienced engineers from around the world.
